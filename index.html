Featured case

Sorting line, drawing to running production

A land-based facility needed a sorting line that could hold grading accuracy at full throughput without adding people. MEST took it from first drawing to commissioning in fourteen weeks, built in Runavík and installed on site.

Scope covered drawing and calculation, stainless steel fabrication, conveyor equipment, guarding and electrical installation. Grading tolerance was verified on site against the customer's own control weights before handover, and the line has run two seasons on scheduled service only.

Featured case

Workboat outfitted for daily site operations

A 17 m aluminium catamaran built and outfitted for feed handling, net inspection and mooring work. Deck layout, crane placement and hydraulics were drawn around the crew's actual working day rather than a standard arrangement.

MEST handled hull outfitting, deck equipment, hydraulic systems, electrical installation and sea trials, with the yard in Skála and service capacity in Runavík covering the first year of operation. Annual maintenance now runs on a fixed window agreed with the customer ahead of each season.
